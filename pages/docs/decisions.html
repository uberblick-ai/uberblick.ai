Decisions

A decision made in a chat is gone by Thursday. In Uberblick a decision is a record: a question, the answer in force, who settled it and when, the reasoning, and every earlier answer underneath. Agents propose, people decide, and the next agent that touches the issue finds the record before it writes a line.

What a record is

A decision topic has one title, the question, and one or more records. The record in force shows its answer first, with the person who decided it and the date; the reasoning and the discussion follow. A later record on the same topic supersedes the earlier one, which stays in the history. Records carry a stage: open, in force, superseded, rejected, withdrawn, or conflict when two answers claim to be in force.

An agent proposes

When an agent hits a choice the pages do not settle, it creates a record with create_doc of kind decision: the question, its proposed answer and the reasoning, with the GitHub issue linked in the prose. The record is open. The agent does not decide; it leaves the stage open and says so in the issue. A record on an existing topic is created as a successor and inherits the title.

A person settles it

In the browser, the topic page shows the open record with an answer editor. Approving puts the record in force under your name and date. Rejecting needs a reason, which is stored. Later, Alter decision on the topic opens a new record with the current answer prefilled, so changing a decision is the same act as making one and the history keeps both. Over MCP the equivalent is set_status, which an agent may use to withdraw its own open proposal but which records a person's answer only when one is given.

find_decisions returns every live record whose prose links the issue or pull request, from the local index, without calling GitHub. Our agent instructions make the preparer, the implementer and the reviewer call it for the issue they work on, so a decision from last month shapes this month's change without anyone remembering it. list_docs with kind: decision lists all topics; the unfiltered listing leaves them out on purpose.

Where decisions show up

On the topic page itself, in All documents on one line with a stage pill, in the sidebar when pinned, and as a condensed block at the end of every page that links to the topic, so a design page shows the decisions that govern it. Archiving a topic archives all its records; restoring brings the topic back first.
