Guides

Four guides to get set up, then four jobs, each shown with a page from the workspace Uberblick is built with. Not a feature tour: what the page looked like before, what the agent and the person did, and what it looks like now.

Start

  • installInstall Uberblick.Three commands and five minutes: the client from Homebrew, a workspace in your project, the editor in your browser. No account.
  • ten minutesYour first ten minutes.One page, one agent, one decision: write a rule, watch the agent read it before it answers, change it, ask again, then find a decision by issue.
  • first workspaceYour first workspace.What two commands put on disk: one SQLite file, one binding, and what to do for a second workspace or none.
  • hubSet up a hub.From a local workspace to one a team edits: Docker, claim, reach, upload, members, connect.

Jobs

  • product intentProduct intent that agents read before they build.A design review becomes ten pages and two decisions; fourteen issues point at them; the loop builds against the pages, not the issue text.
  • decisionsDecisions that stay decided."Support for Mermaid": the record, the successor that chose ablauf, and the history that stays when the question comes back.
  • reportsReports with the numbers inside.The delivery report and the CI health report: a skill writes records, charts read them, a card carries the one recommendation.
  • flowsFlows and screens drawn as text.The agent loop as an ablauf flow with its gates, and a screen from a "Web UI:" page as a wireframe, source beside drawing.

Research that does not get lost in chat is a fifth job; it gets a page when we have a research page in our own workspace worth showing.
